# Moraloop 30-day Instagram plan

One Reel a day at 7:00 PM (your local time). The videos are vertical 1080×1920, all under 3 minutes, so they work as Instagram Reels.

Captions stay neutral on every dilemma (see `CLAUDE.md`), and each one ends with the same call to action as the end card.

**Approval:** 15 posts use creative the PDF marks as approved or saved favorites, plus the original ad and the new storyline. The 13 marked *Pending approval* come from the PDF's review inventory, so confirm those before they go live.
