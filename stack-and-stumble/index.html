He wants the top.

A stick figure is trying to climb out of your well. He runs, jumps, climbs and grabs onto your pieces, even the one that is still falling. Every piece you land shakes him. Slam one next to him to knock him off, drop one on his head, or move and spin the falling piece to throw him off it. If he reaches the top, you lose.
