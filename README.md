## Stack & Stumble

A Tetris game where a stick figure tries to climb your pieces and escape out the top of the well. Open `stack-and-stumble/index.html` in a browser to play — no build step.

- Arrow keys move and rotate, Space hard-drops, C holds, P pauses. On phones, use the on-screen buttons.
- He runs, jumps, climbs blocks, grabs and rides the falling piece, dodges pieces, drills his way out when boxed in, and teleports to the top of the stack after 25 seconds stuck.
- Every piece you land shakes him. Drop pieces on him, slam them beside him, or move and spin the piece he's riding to keep him down. If he reaches the top, you lose.
