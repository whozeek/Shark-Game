# Shark-Game
A fun game to play where you get points and dodge sharks 

## Play
Open `index.html` in any browser (no install or build needed). Works with keyboard, mouse, or touch.

## How it works
- **Move:** arrow keys / WASD, or hold and drag with the mouse or a finger
- **Pearls** are worth 10 points; rare **golden pearls** are worth 50 but vanish fast
- **Combos:** grab pearls quickly to raise your multiplier (up to ×5)
- **Lives:** you have 3 hearts, with a short invincibility window after each hit
- **Power-ups:** 🛡 shield (blocks one bite), ⏱ slow-mo (sharks slow down), 🧲 magnet (pulls pearls to you)
- **Sharks** get tougher every 20 seconds:
  - grey **cruisers** swim across the screen
  - red-eyed **hunters** (level 2+) chase you
  - **dashers** (level 3+) flash a red warning lane, then charge through it
- Your best score is saved in the browser. **P**/Esc pauses, **M** mutes.
