Shark Game

You're a little fish in a big ocean. Eat fish food to earn coins — and don't get eaten by sharks!

  • Move: arrow keys / WASD, or drag with mouse or finger
  • Fish food floats in from the right — each bite is 1 coin, golden flakes are 5. Eat fast to build a combo (up to ×5)
  • Power-ups: 🛡 shield · ⏱ slow-mo · 🧲 magnet
  • Watch out for red hunters that chase you and dashers that charge after a warning
  • P / Esc to pause · M to mute
